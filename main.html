Movie Summary

3 Idiots is a comedy-drama about three engineering students, Rancho, Farhan, and Raju, who experience friendship, academic pressure, and personal growth while studying at an engineering college. The story encourages students to follow excellence and passion rather than chasing success alone.

Main Actors

  • Aamir Khan as Rancho
  • R. Madhavan as Farhan
  • Sharman Joshi as Raju
  • Kareena Kapoor Khan as Pia
  • Boman Irani as Viru "Virus" Sahastrabuddhe

The actors bring humor, emotion, and friendship to the story.
